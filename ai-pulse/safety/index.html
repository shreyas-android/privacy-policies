AIniverse – Child Safety Standards

App: AIniverse: Models & Playground (Android package com.androidai.ai.threads.android) · Last updated: November 2026

AIniverse: Models & Playground (“AIniverse”) has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to the AIniverse app and to everything we publish or operate through it.

What is prohibited

The following is strictly prohibited in AIniverse, in any form, including text, images, audio, video and AI-generated or synthetic material:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or any sexual content involving a minor.
  • The sexualization of minors, including requests to an AI model to describe, create or role-play such content.
  • Grooming, enticing or soliciting a minor, and sextortion.
  • Child trafficking or any other exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Content that promotes, depicts or encourages harm to minors, or that links to or advertises any of the above.

Using AIniverse to look for, request, produce, store or share any of this is a violation of these standards and of our terms.

How AIniverse works, and what that means for safety

AIniverse is a tool for chatting with AI models and exploring AI models, research and news. The current version has no feed or space where people publish content for other users to see. What is shown to everyone (AI News, model, benchmark and skill catalogs) is published by AIniverse itself or comes from official company sources. Messages typed in the Playground are sent to the AI model the person chooses in order to get a reply.

Reporting a concern

  • In the app: open Settings and choose Send Feedback. It opens a message to our child safety contact below, with the app version and device details attached.
  • By email: write to the child safety contact below at any time, with a link or a description of what you saw.

We review every report about child safety as a priority.

Enforcement and legal compliance

When content or conduct that sexually exploits or endangers a child is identified, we remove it, block the access involved, and keep the information needed for an investigation. Where the law requires it, we report to the relevant regional or national authority, including the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and we cooperate with law enforcement.

Age requirement

AIniverse is intended for users aged 18 years and older. People under 18 are not permitted to create an account or use the app.
