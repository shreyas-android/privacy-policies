AIniverse – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

AIniverse (“we”, “our”, “us”) is an app for exploring AI: a catalog of AI models, tools, papers and benchmarks, AI news, and playgrounds where you can chat with AI models, run prompts and agents, and make AI voice and video. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information (only if you sign in): your email address, name and profile picture from Google Sign-In, plus any username, bio or images you add to your profile. You can use most of the app as a guest without an account.
  • Content you create: prompts, collections, skills and agents you save or publish, ratings, bookmarks, follows and likes, and connectors you link.
  • What you send to AI features: the text, files and voice input you give a chat, prompt, agent or generator, so it can be answered (see “AI features” below).
  • Feedback and reports: messages you send from the in-app Send Feedback / Report form, an optional contact you choose to add, and the app version and platform.
  • Device and usage data: app interactions, crash reports and diagnostics, device model and OS version, and a push notification token.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads (see “Advertising”).

Playground chat history, downloaded on-device models and their outputs are kept on your device and are not uploaded to us.

Microphone

If you tap the microphone to dictate, the app asks for microphone permission and uses your device’s speech recognition to turn speech into text. We do not record or store audio.

AI features

When you use a cloud AI model, what you send is processed by the provider that runs that model, for example Google (Gemini, via Firebase AI), OpenRouter, Groq or another provider you choose, or a server you connect yourself. Those providers handle it under their own privacy policies and may log it, so do not include passwords or other secrets. On-device models run entirely on your phone. AI output can be wrong or offensive: you can report it from the flag on any AI screen.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: sign-in, your profile, saved content, and running the AI features you use.
  • To send notifications you have switched on (for example AI news).
  • To fix crashes and improve the app (Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics).
  • To review feedback and reports, enforce our rules, and improve content filtering and moderation.
  • To show ads (Google AdMob).

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google / Firebase: sign-in, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging (notifications) and Firebase AI.
  • Google AdMob: ads, which may use your advertising ID.
  • AI model providers: only what you send to an AI feature, as described above.
  • Authorities, where required by law (for example reports of child sexual abuse material).

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID and approximate location to choose them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s Google settings. See how Google uses data in ads.

Security and storage

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Supabase and Google. Access to your account data is limited to you through sign-in and database access rules.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open the Profile tab and tap Delete Account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account and its data from our servers straight away. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account. Feedback and reports you sent are kept without your name or account.

Children

AIniverse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. See our Child Safety Standards.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
