AIniverse – Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your AIniverse account and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open AIniverse and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted from our servers straight away, and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email avengers14.blogger@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject “AIniverse account deletion request”.

We confirm the request came from the account’s email address, then delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile (name, username, bio, profile and banner images).
  • Content you created: prompts, collections, skills, agents and any posts.
  • Your bookmarks, likes, follows, ratings, notification settings and device push tokens.
  • Connectors you linked, including their stored access tokens.

What is kept

  • Feedback and reports you sent from the app, without your name or account attached, so we can still act on safety reports.
  • Reports other people made about content, as needed for moderation and legal obligations.
  • Database backups, which roll over and are gone within 30 days.

Chats and models stored only on your phone are not on our servers; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

See also our Privacy Policy.
